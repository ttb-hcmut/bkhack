## Catpuccin

One of the developers was obsessed with the Catpuccin color scheme and wanted to apply it beyond their Linux computer.

## Devbook

Another one of the developers was obsessed with reading and writing academic papers, and was inspired to incorporate some beautiful colors they came across in the making of the devbook. 

State Machine Blue (`blue`, `stm`) was picked from the front cover of Phan Thị Tươi's Giáo trình Trình Biên Dịch (Textbook for the course Compiler Construction), a book which one of the developers had a fond time reading. These colors were also indirectly inspired by the diagrams and syntax highlighting in the paper "MacoCaml: Staging Composable and Compilable Macros" by Ningning Xie et. al. which one of the developers had a dearly pleasant time reading. The name is also a reference to The Tatami Time Machine Blues (Japanese: 四畳半タイムマシンブルース), a series which one the developers had a fond time watching.

Category Cream (`cream`, `ghost`) was picked from the beautiful diagrams of Craig Ferguson's blog (https://www.craigfe.io), where the topics of OCaml modules, signatures, functors, and categories were discussed, one of the developers was deeply mesmerised.

## Style layering

There are multiple semantics layers:
 - Syntactical layer: this is the catpuccin color palette as-is.
 - Widget semantic layer: these are variables that denote roles in the composition of a user interface.
